## Summary

✅ **SDK has NO internal timeout on `onPermissionRequest`.**

The `onPermissionRequest` callback's returned Promise is awaited indefinitely by the SDK. No `setTimeout`, `Promise.race`, or `AbortController` wraps the handler invocation anywhere in the SDK distribution. The no-timeout path (Noble Six's Branch A recommendation in ADR-9) is fully supported end-to-end at the SDK layer.

## Implication for ADR-9

**Branch A (no-timeout) is ACCEPTED.** Remove `timeoutMs` default from `promptUserForPermission` signature. The 60-second default parameter and internal `setTimeout`/`Promise.race` should be deleted. Replace timeout with `permission.cancelled` message and AbortSignal (Q3 settled). Optional UX safety net: passive log warning after 10 minutes (not a hard timeout).
